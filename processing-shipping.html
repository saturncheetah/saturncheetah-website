01 · Processing

When production begins

Processing begins after payment and final design approval. Regular customised orders are generally processed within 5–7 days, subject to product availability.

Bulk orders are generally processed within 10–15 days when the selected goods are available. Larger quantities, complex branding, stock availability or artwork changes may affect the timeline; any expected change will be discussed with you.

02 · Shipping

Before your order travels

Shipping charges and the estimated courier delivery time are shared before order confirmation. Charges may vary according to destination, package weight and courier availability.

Orders are dispatched only after full payment. Tracking details are shared after dispatch. Courier delivery time begins after dispatch and is separate from processing time.

03 · Bulk payment

A transparent 60/40 process

A 60% advance confirms the bulk order and begins production. After completion, we share photographs and videos of the finished goods.

The remaining 40% must be paid before dispatch, delivery or handover. Bulk samples are available on a paid basis.

04 · Delivery support

If the courier needs attention

Courier delays can occasionally occur outside our control, but we will help you follow up using the available tracking information.

Please provide a complete delivery address and working telephone number. Additional charges caused by an incorrect address, refused delivery or unsuccessful delivery attempt may need to be paid before reshipping.

Need an order-specific estimate?

Tell us the product, quantity, destination and required date. We will confirm the applicable processing and shipping details before you place the order.
