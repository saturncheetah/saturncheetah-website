Quick answers

Need a quick answer?

Choose a question below and we’ll point you in the right direction.

How much does a custom T-shirt cost?

Custom 180 GSM T-shirts range from ₹799–₹999, while 240 GSM T-shirts range from ₹899–₹1,199. The final price depends on the design, number of prints, print sizes and other customisation requirements.

Chat with us on WhatsApp ↗
Can I order just one T-shirt?

Yes. Individual customised T-shirts can be ordered from one piece.

Customise a Tee →
Can you help me create a design?

Yes. Share your idea or reference images with us on WhatsApp, and we’ll help you develop a design direction suitable for printing.

Chat with us on WhatsApp ↗
Which sizes and fits are available?

Our garments are unisex and available from XS to XXL in regular and oversized fits. If you prefer a closer fit, you may choose one size smaller.

How long does an order take?

Regular customised orders generally take 5–7 days to process after payment and final design approval, subject to product availability. Delivery time is additional and depends on the destination and courier availability.

Do you accept bulk orders?

Yes. Bulk orders start from 25 pieces and generally take 10–12 days to process after payment and final design approval, subject to product availability.

Request a Bulk Quote →
How do payment and delivery work?

We confirm the final price, availability, processing time and delivery details before confirming your order. Orders are dispatched after full payment, and tracking details are shared after dispatch.

Can I return or exchange a customised product?

No. Customised products are made specifically to your confirmed size, design and requirements, so returns, exchanges and refunds are not available after approval.
