Quick answers

Before you message us

Can I order one T-shirt?

Yes. Personal customisation starts from one piece for the 180 GSM regular and 240 GSM oversized options.

What is the difference between the two tees?

The 180 GSM tee has a familiar regular fit. The 240 GSM tee has a heavier, relaxed oversized silhouette.

Can I send my own design?

Yes. Open WhatsApp from the customisation pathway, then attach your ready design, reference or idea.

How do ordering and payment work?

Enquiries continue on WhatsApp. Feasibility, final price, timeline and payment are confirmed directly; this website has no payment gateway.
