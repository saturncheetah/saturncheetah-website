## Recommended conversion-focused order

1. **Announcement:** “Custom tees from 1 piece • Bulk orders welcome.”
2. **Compact header:** brand, menu, labelled WhatsApp action.
3. **Hero:** copy first on every viewport; one-piece proof; primary WhatsApp
   CTA; compact product proof; supporting image.
4. **Choose your path:** Personal/custom from one piece versus Bulk/wider
   range.
5. **Choose your tee:** 180 GSM regular versus 240 GSM oversized with concise
   fit/use comparison.
6. **How it works:** Choose tee → share idea → confirm → print.
7. **Real printing proof:** poster-first video with explicit play.
8. **Finished work and design inspiration:** privacy-safe proof with
   “Send a design like this” actions and Instagram link.
9. **Genuine reviews:** render only when approved reviews and publication
   permission exist; otherwise omit the section completely.
10. **Bulk pathway:** wider range and a dedicated WhatsApp quote action.
11. **FAQ and reassurance:** one piece, artwork, no gateway, final
    quote/timeline, and only confirmed delivery/business facts.
12. **Short WhatsApp enquiry:** progressive essentials first, optional details
    second.
13. **Footer:** WhatsApp, Instagram, brand, and verified policy links when
    available.

## Rationale

The sequence answers the mobile buyer's questions in decision order: “Can you
make one?” → “Which tee?” → “How does it work?” → “Can I trust the result?” →
“What if I need bulk?” → “What happens when I message?” The personal route
stays simple while the bulk route remains available without overwhelming the
primary one-piece customer.
