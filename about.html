About us

Why Saturn Cheetah?

Founded by Dishali Mehta, Saturn Cheetah began with a simple belief: clothing can be a form of self-expression.

Cheetah is how we create.

Quick, but never careless. Your idea moves from a thought to a finished design with speed, accuracy and care—so the T-shirt reaches you when the feeling still matters.

Saturn is why it stays.

A personalised tee holds a piece of you in time. A thought, a memory, a mood or something you have always wanted to say becomes visible—and every time you wear it, the experience becomes yours again.

The thought behind it

A personalised T-shirt can hold a thought, a memory, a mood—or a part of you that words cannot always capture.

For an introvert, it can speak before you have to. For an extrovert, it can keep speaking on the days when you do not quite feel like yourself. That is the timeless experience: expressing who you are through what you wear, without needing to find the perfect words.
